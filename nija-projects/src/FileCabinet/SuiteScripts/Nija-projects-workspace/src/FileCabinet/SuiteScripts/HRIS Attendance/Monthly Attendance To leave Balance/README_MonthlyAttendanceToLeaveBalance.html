Monthly Attendance to Leave Balance

Comp-Off Days Accrual & Processing Workflow

Process Overview

This module handles the consolidated monthly payroll run and automatically accrues earned employee compensatory leave times. When the payroll runs, it queries employee monthly attendance, displays their earned comp-off totals, and submits them to accrue in their compensatory leave balances.

Map/Reduce Processing

File: njt_monthlyotandlopcreation_mrs.js

Processes the serialized checks in the background. In addition to creating salary journals and unpaid leaves, it executes a leave balance search and updates the balance records:

// 1. Search for active Compensatory Off (Type: 11) leave balances
var leavebalancesql = "SELECT id, custrecord_hris_lvbal_leave_balance_cred, custrecord_hris_lvbal_available_leave_ba, custrecord_hris_lvbal_annual_leave_bal FROM customrecord_hris_leavebalance WHERE custrecord_hris_lvbal_employee_name = " + empid + " AND custrecord_hris_lvbal_leave_type = 11 AND isinactive = 'F'";

// 2. Accumulate the comp-off days to the credited, annual, and available fields
var newCredited = leavebalancecredited + compoffdays;
var newAvailable = availableleavebal + compoffdays;
var newAnnual = annualleavebal + compoffdays;

Consistent balances updates: To maintain balance sheet integrity, comp-off days are added simultaneously to Credited Balance, Annual Balance, and Available Balance fields.
