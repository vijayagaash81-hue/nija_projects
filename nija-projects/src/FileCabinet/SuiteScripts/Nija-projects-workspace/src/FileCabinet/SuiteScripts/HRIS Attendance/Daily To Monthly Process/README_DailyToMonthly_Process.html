Consolidated Attendance Approval

Daily Timesheet Entry to Monthly Attendance Generation

Process Workflow

This process provides payroll administrators with an approval board to consolidate daily timesheet entry items, review working durations, and trigger the batch creation of official Monthly Attendance records.

1
Search Filters Definition

The administrator defines consolidation filters on the Suitelet form, specifying the target Month, Year, Subsidiary, and Location.

2
Consolidated Timesheet Grid View

The Suitelet queries the daily database and displays a consolidated sublist line per employee, summarizing their total Present Days, Absent Days, OT Hours, and computed Comp-Off/Roundoff values.

3
Employee Selection & Submission

The administrator marks the checkboxes next to the employee entries they wish to approve for payroll and submits the form.

4
Map/Reduce Batch Record Creation

The Suitelet launches a Map/Reduce script in the background. The script processes each selected employee in parallel, creating or updating their corresponding Monthly Attendance record in NetSuite.

Note: By processing the record creation via a background Map/Reduce script, administrators avoid browser time-out issues when approving payroll for hundreds of employees simultaneously.
